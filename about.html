06 / About

Making coding feel approachable.

CodePlay is a college Community Engagement Project for beginner students at Janseva Hindi High School, Kalyan, Maharashtra.

The need

Coding should feel less like a wall of theory.

CodePlay responds to the challenge of learning coding concepts mainly through theory and syntax by making space for practice, play, and problem solving.

The approach

Learn → Play → Solve → Earn → Progress

Short explanations and interactive activities help beginner students build confidence through doing.
